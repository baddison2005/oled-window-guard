# OLED Window Guard 1.3.0

Choose where the on-screen move warning appears under **Safety & alerts → Warning position**.

- **Top left**, **Top centre** and **Top right** anchor the warning near the top of each affected display. Top right is the default.
- **Random within top 25%** chooses a fresh position for each warning to reduce repeated use of the same spot. On shorter displays, the card stays near the top and varies horizontally to keep the full warning visible.

The DMG contains a signed and Apple-notarised universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Open the DMG and drag OLED Window Guard to Applications. A ZIP and SHA-256 checksums are also available.
